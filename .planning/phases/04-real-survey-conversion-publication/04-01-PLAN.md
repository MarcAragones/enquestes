<objective>
Give the pipeline a default column-selection heuristic based on absolute distinct-value count (D-01, D-02), keep it strictly independent of the two exclusion filters that already exist (D-03), and make every exclusion it performs visible and reversible through an explicit report plus an override flag (D-04).

Purpose: v1.0's real export is 2000 rows x 320 columns. Requiring the operator to hand-enumerate every kept column in `--columns` is what makes converting the 2-5 real exports impractical, so PUB-01 cannot be satisfied without this. The worked example from the phase discussion is the target: a 0-10 rating question plus a "no ho se" option has 12 distinct values and must survive the cutoff automatically, while a 40-country "quin pais ets" column is excluded by default and brought back by name only when the operator judges it worth keeping.

Output: `scripts/pipeline/infer.py` gains `MAX_DISTINCT_VALUES`, `is_high_cardinality_column`, `high_cardinality_columns`, and `format_high_cardinality_report`; `scripts/convert_enquesta.py` gains `--max-cardinality` and `--include-columns`, makes `--columns` optional, and prints the D-04 report; `scripts/pipeline_selftest.py` gains regression coverage including one end-to-end conversion run; `scripts/README.md` documents the new default.

Spec-less phase note: all three edge-probe rows (PUB-01, PUB-02, PUB-05) came back unclassified and are carried in `must_haves.assumptions` as explicit flagged assumptions. They are NOT resolved truths and must not be treated as verified predicates.
</objective>
