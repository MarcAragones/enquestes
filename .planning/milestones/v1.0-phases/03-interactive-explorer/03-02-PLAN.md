<objective>
Turn the tracer's bare explorer into the app-shell-wrapped page CONTEXT.md describes: one compact header row (title, back-link, dark-mode toggle) above the canvas, and a collapsed-by-default data dictionary a visitor can open to find out what `recomanaria` actually means.

**As a** visitor exploring a survey I did not design, **I want to** read what each field means and get back to the catalog in one click, **so that** I can build a chart I actually understand instead of guessing at column names.

Purpose: the tracer proved the data path but left the page's chrome minimal and its fields undocumented. Without the dictionary the six raw Catalan column names are the visitor's only clue what they are charting; without a stable header the back-link is the page's only navigation and the survey identity is invisible.

Output: `src/components/ExplorerHeader.tsx`, `src/components/DataDictionary.tsx`, and the `ExplorerPage.tsx` wiring that renders both.
</objective>

|----------|-------------|

| T-03-09 | Tampering | Styling or DOM injected into `<GraphicWalker />`'s internals | low | mitigate | D-06 prohibition enforced: the dictionary is a sibling panel and the header is a sibling row; neither queries, wraps, or overrides GraphicWalker's DOM. Theme wiring only ever goes through a documented prop, never a style override (Task 1) |
</threat_model>
