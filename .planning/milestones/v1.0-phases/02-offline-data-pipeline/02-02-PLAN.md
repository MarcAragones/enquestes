## Phase Goal

**Goal (verbatim from ROADMAP.md Phase 2):** Real and mock survey data can be safely and correctly converted into the Parquet/JSON artifacts the app consumes.

> MVP mode note: this Goal line is not in `As a / I want to / so that` user-story form. It is reproduced verbatim rather than invented. Run `/gsd mvp-phase 02` if a formal user story is wanted on the ROADMAP line.

<objective>
Take the conversion spine proven in plan 02-01 and make it survive a real export: a privacy checklist that catches the quasi-identifiers a uniqueness ratio alone cannot see, and an input loader that copes with `.xlsx` files, Windows-codepage CSVs and spreadsheets carrying a stray title or total row.

This is the "validation and error states" expansion of the tracer, not a new spine. It changes no CLI flag and no output contract — the same `uv run scripts/convert_enquesta.py` invocation gains depth behind `run_privacy_checklist` and `load_table`.

D-04 stays honoured: the `.xlsx` branch is a file-extension branch on one export shape, not a multi-vendor abstraction layer.

Purpose: DATA-03's success criterion is "flags potential quasi-identifiers, not just name/email columns". Plan 02-01 only shipped the near-unique check. This plan closes that gap.

Output: a complete `scripts/pipeline/privacy.py`, a new `scripts/pipeline/load.py`, and extended self-test coverage.
</objective>

Emit at most one finding per combination, never one per group. Skip the scan entirely when fewer than two dimension columns exist, or when the frame has zero rows, and record that skip as an unevaluated entry rather than as a pass.
